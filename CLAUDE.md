# Mechakov notes for Claude

- Prop hunt + extraction game: three.js client (`client/`), Node `ws` server (`server/`), shared rules in `shared/` used by both (the client predicts movement with the same `stepBody`).
- Checks: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build`. CI runs all three.
- Workflow agreed with the owner: when a PR's CI is green, merge it yourself and move on to the next stage.
- Roadmap: 1) multiplayer core (done) 2) raid loot, extraction, hideout upgrades (done) 3) maps, balance, quick play, prop bots (done) 4) anti-cheat basics, Docker/deploy config (done); the owner hasn't picked a host yet, see DEPLOY.md.
- UI text is Korean.
- Sounds are synthesized with Web Audio in `client/src/sfx.ts` (no audio files); N mutes. M opens the full map (`client/src/mapview.ts`, shows only your own position).
- The game is free-for-all (owner's call): `Room.hunters` is false by default, so everyone is a prop, the raid starts straight in the `hunt` phase (hide + hunt time) with exits open, everyone can hurt everyone, and `roundEnd` has no winning team. Setting `room.hunters = true` brings back the old hunters-vs-props mode (tests use it).
- Characters (`shared/characters.ts`) are the owner's to design; each has slots: health and speed multipliers (folded into `maxHp`/`speedMult`), a disguise ability (`prop`: E turns into map props; `invisible`: standing still `INVISIBLE_AFTER` seconds hides you from everyone else's snapshots until you move or attack, tracked as `Player.stillFor`; `decoy`: E leaves a copy, a `Player` with `decoyOf` set that lives in `players` so every attack can hit it, snapshots it like a real player, is left out of the roster, round counts and extraction, and pops at the first hit), a weapon (rocket, grenade or hitscan) and an optional right-click/Q skill (`leap`: hold to charge, let go to throw yourself in a grenade-like arc on `vx/vy/vz`; `BodyState.charge` marks the leap until landing so prediction replays it, and the first player you fly into or land on takes the hit). Current: 로켓병 (prop disguise, rockets), 척탄병 (invisible, TF2-style grenade launcher that arcs, bounces and goes off on a direct hit or a fuse, leap skill) and a stand-in `decoyTest` for trying the decoy until the owner gives it a character. Picked with C in the lobby or hideout (`character` message, kept on `Profile.character`). Bots get a random one but don't fight.
- Testing alone: press B in the lobby to add prop bots (`server/src/bot.ts`). Solo also has a practice range (`range` theme, `rangeMap` in `shared/map.ts`): the hideout's `range` station (F) sends `practice` and `Room.practice` swaps to it, its `leave` station goes back. It has target dummies (`Player.dummy`, standing or strafing, heal 3 s after a hit, reset at 0 hp, drawn by `createDummyMesh`), walls of three heights and a row of every prop; fighting is always on there, characters can be changed with C, and no raid starts from it. Your hits send you a `dmg` message, shown as floating damage numbers. `Player.fake` covers decoys and dummies for roster, counts and extraction.
- `tests/physics.test.ts` flood-fills every map to check spawns and exits are reachable; keep it passing when changing map layouts.
- Solo mode runs `Room` in the browser (`client/src/local.ts`) with `room.autoStart = false` (owner's calls: between raids you walk around the `hideout` map (`shared/map.ts`, stations: stash lockers, workbench, raid door; bots are hidden there), and the round ends once the person's raid is decided), so `server/src/room.ts` must stay free of Node-only imports. `npm run build:web` makes the single-file solo page published as a claude.ai artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/HNCrFpqRfUqzo3xroXEpfe); republish it after gameplay changes.
- Raid maps are 180 m across (`HALF = 90` in `shared/map.ts`): a 3x3 grid of areas split by two roads each way (`ROADS`), the middle open for the hunters' spawn and eight separate buildings or outdoor areas around it, laid out per theme (`warehouse`/`office`/`yard` functions) with props chosen to fit each area. Each area is written around its own middle via `b.at(x, z)` (a `Local` view of the `Builder`). Place walls and props only through `Builder` (`shared/layouts.ts`), which keeps doorways, exits, roads and the middle clear; `building()` adds outer walls with lintels over the doors, and the client draws its floor, roof and a lamp. The client's sun shadow follows the camera (`World.follow`). Sprint stamina lives in `BodyState` so the client predicts it with the same `stepBody`; snapshots carry it for reconciliation.
- Props are drawn from detailed models in `client/src/models.ts` (parts with vertex colors merged into one geometry per type, front facing +z; `rot` is 0-3 quarter turns so things face into rooms). Collision is still the box in `shared/props.ts`, so keep models inside it. Warehouse racks and yard containers are drawn there too, from their wall boxes: rack style comes from wall height (2.4 small-parts shelving, 3.2 general, 4.2 pallet racking), 2.5 m thin walls in the warehouse are wire fencing, and racks get floor stripes and aisle signs.
- Owner's calls on layouts: orderly rows rather than random scatter, things that block sight (racks, high cubicle walls, fire doors, containers) so you can't see across the map, and not too many props (about 300 to 600 per map; the 180 m maps sit around 400 to 650).
- Props have TF2-style rockets (click): server-simulated projectiles in `Room` (`fireRocket`/`moveRockets`/`explode`), weak damage to everyone but the shooter once fighting starts (props can fight each other), strong knockback to everyone in the blast including the shooter (rocket jumps; invisible `skyFence` boxes above the outer walls keep jumps inside the map). Knockback lives in `BodyState.vx/vz` so prediction replays it; flight tuning is in `shared/constants.ts` (`ROCKET_*`), damage, cooldown, knockback and clip per character: rockets and grenades hold 3 shots (`clip`) and the whole clip refills `reload` seconds after the last shot (`ammoAfter` in `shared/characters.ts`, used by both server and client; the client shows pips under the crosshair). Rockets leave after a short wind-up (`ROCKET_DELAY`, held as `pendingRocket` on the player) and props get a ring crosshair. Client effects are in `client/src/rockets.ts`. Bag items can be dropped from the Tab inventory (click a slot, `drop` message; Tab frees the mouse). A dead player's bag spills on the floor as loot (`dropBag`); a hunter who kills a prop takes it instead, since hunters can't pick things up.
